TIDAL BUDS: Factory to Doorstep

Discover how our waterproof earbuds transition from an innovative concept into a fully manufactured, high-performance product ready for your daily routine.

Precision Manufacturing & Quality Control

Every single pair of Tidal Buds is put through rigorous pressure testing in specialized vacuum chambers. This ensures that the one-piece sealed shell can withstand deep submersions and intense daily wear without letting a single drop of moisture inside.

Our assembly lines combine automated robotic precision for micro-circuit soldering with expert human inspection to guarantee absolute comfort, durability, and peerless sound fidelity.

Sustainable Materials & Sourcing

We believe in building technology that respects our planet. All plastics used in our charging cases and packaging are sourced from recycled polymers, drastically cutting down our carbon footprint without compromising structural integrity or resilience.

Production Quick Specs

Assembly: Clean-room automated sealing

Testing: 100% IPX8 pressure-tested

Packaging: 100% recyclable, eco-friendly materials

Fulfillment: Direct-to-consumer carbon-neutral shipping

Global Quality Assurance

Before any batch leaves our facility, random samples undergo stress trials involving extreme temperature shifts, drop tests, and chemical exposure tests to guarantee they easily outperform market expectations.
