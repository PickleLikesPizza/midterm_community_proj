What Makes Our Design Revolutionary

Most earbuds are only "water resistant." Ours are fully waterproof, so you can swim, surf, shower, or really do anything with them.

No Open Ports

Charging ports are the main way water gets in, so our earbuds have none. They charge wirelessly in the case, and the shell is sealed in one piece.

Swim Mode

Sound travels differently underwater. We added a swim mode that senses when you go under the water and adjusts the audio so music stays clear.

Parent-Approved Privacy

Engineered so that only you can hear your audio loud and clear, preventing anyone else nearby from hearing your music or shower concerts.

Ultra-Secure Ergonomic Fit

Our innovative shape locks safely and comfortably into your ear canal, guaranteeing they stay put no matter how active your routine gets.

Built to Last

Constructed with premium, heavy-duty materials designed to resist wear and tear, ensuring your earbuds last for years without losing sound quality.

Smart Investment

Get elite, professional-grade waterproof audio technology at a fraction of the cost of inferior competitor brands.
