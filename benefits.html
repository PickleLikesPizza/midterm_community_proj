What Makes Our Design Revolutionary

Most earbuds are only "water resistant." Ours are fully waterproof, so you can swim, surf, shower, or really do anything with them.

No Open Ports

Charging ports are the main way water gets in, so our earbuds have none. They charge wirelessly in the case, and the shell is sealed in one piece.

Swim Mode

Sound travels differently underwater. We added a swim mode that senses when you go under the water and adjusts the audio so music stays clear.
